My "Number 1" Web-Hosting Site

The best web-hosting site, in my opinion, is Cloudways. Cloudways service is somewhat cheap but has a multitude of other benefits. Firstly, it has the highest storage limit of all and the highest bandwidth limit, capping out at 9 Terabytes. Secondly, you can have unlimited visits and sites from the cheapest plan. Lastly, it had a high level of security and support. One thing I didn’t like was that staging was an add-on that needed to be bought but, in this case, the pros outweigh the cons. A close second was Bluehost, having almost everything Cloudways did. The one thing that gave Cloudways the win was that it had unlimited visits and sites while Bluehost was sitting at 100 sites, which is a lot but nowhere near infinite.
